Privacy Policy

Custom Rings  ·  Last updated: August 27, 2026

This policy explains what Custom Rings processes, why it is processed, when information leaves your device, and the choices available to you.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your complete ring collection is stored locally on your devices. This includes:

  • Ring and cluster names, targets, schedules, colors, and progress
  • Daily history and streak information
  • Reminder, widget, Siri, and app preferences

This local information is shared between the Custom Rings iPhone app, Apple Watch app, and widgets using Apple's App Groups and Watch connectivity features. Local information remains until you remove it or uninstall the app.

Accounts and Social Features

Social features are optional and require Sign in with Apple. When you use them, Custom Rings and its service provider, Supabase, process:

  • Your Sign in with Apple user identifier and any email address Apple provides, which may be a private relay address
  • Your chosen username and profile information
  • Friend requests, friendships, mute and notification preferences
  • Shared ring configuration and daily progress
  • Challenges, challenge standings, nudges, and activity-feed events
  • An Apple Push Notification service device token when you enable notifications

This information is used only to provide authentication, sharing, friends, challenges, activity feeds, account management, and notifications. It is associated with your Custom Rings account so these features can work.

Apple Health Data

If you create an Auto-Track ring, Custom Rings requests read-only access to the Apple Health category you select, such as steps, active calories, exercise minutes, stand hours, distance, flights climbed, sleep, water, or time in daylight.

  • Raw Apple Health samples are read and processed on your device.
  • Custom Rings does not upload the underlying Health samples.
  • If you explicitly share an Auto-Track ring, its ring name, selected Health category, daily total, completion status, and streak information are sent to Supabase and made visible to friends you have approved.

You can revoke Health access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Custom Rings.

Service Providers

  • Supabase provides account authentication, database storage, server functions, and social-feature infrastructure.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, StoreKit purchases, push notifications, and opt-in App Store analytics and diagnostics.

Information may be processed in the United States. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

In-App Purchases

Custom Rings offers a one-time Pro upgrade through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Analytics & Advertising

Custom Rings does not contain advertising SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Apple may provide aggregate App Store analytics and opt-in crash diagnostics to the developer.

Retention and Account Deletion

Server-side account and social information is retained while your account is active or as needed to operate and secure the service. You may request permanent account deletion using the contact address below. Account deletion removes your Custom Rings profile, friendships, shared ring progress, challenges, activity events, and notification tokens from the service. Local ring data on your device is not removed by account deletion.

Your Choices

  • Social features are optional; the core ring tracker can be used without an account.
  • You choose which individual rings, if any, are shared with approved friends.
  • You can revoke Health access and notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can unshare rings, remove friends, sign out, or request account deletion using the contact address below.

Security

We use platform authentication, access-control policies, encrypted network connections, and service-provider security controls intended to protect server-side information. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Custom Rings is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided account information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
